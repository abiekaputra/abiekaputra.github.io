Satset / Engineering notes

Abstention is a product feature.

Decision
Use deterministic BM25 retrieval and evidence extraction so ranking and citations remain inspectable.
Validation
A quality lab measures recall, ranking, citation coverage, and latency against known-answer cases.
Limit
It does not fabricate an answer when the curated local source set lacks evidence.
